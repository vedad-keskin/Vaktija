# Home artwork

Eight original, individually authored vector scenes: `lastthird.svg`, `fajr.svg`,
`sunrise.svg`, `dhuhr.svg`, `asr.svg`, `maghrib.svg`, `isha.svg`, `midnight.svg`.
They share a pointed arch, emerald valley, river, mosque silhouette and gold rim.
Asr places the sun high on the right with a long diagonal ground shadow. These
are symbolic time-of-day illustrations, not predictions of the moon's phase.

Each SVG hides fine details at a rendered width of 64px or less. No icon is a
crop of the hero, and neither the concept screenshot nor an icon sheet is used.

The original hero is `../ornaments/mosque-valley.webp`, generated with the built-in
image generation tool and optimized to WebP. The same scene sits behind ivory
text and an emerald contrast scrim in both themes.

## Hero generation prompt

Create an original wide landscape illustration asset for a prayer-time app countdown panel, no UI, no typography, no numbers, no border. Painterly yet clean elegant editorial illustration: layered Bosnian mountain ridges, a calm river or lake in a valley, a graceful Ottoman mosque with one main dome, small domes and tall slender minarets anchored at the lower far right, a few dark cypress trees on the left. Deep midnight emerald / blue-green sky, forest green mountains and silhouettes, restrained warm honey gold light edging the distant hills and mosque dome. Dawn sun low near the lower-right third, soft golden horizon and reflected light on the lake. Composition is critical: extremely wide panorama, about 3:1; the upper two-thirds and central 40 percent must be dark quiet open emerald sky with very little detail so ivory countdown text will read over it. The terrain, mosque and golden light mainly occupy the bottom third and far side edges. Rich but restrained; scenery vivid and luminous at the edges, calm behind center. Related to refined Islamic manuscript landscape miniatures with modern simple silhouettes, no geometric repeating pattern, no literal calligraphy, no people. Fully opaque artwork, no transparent background. Output a finished original illustration, not an interface mockup.
