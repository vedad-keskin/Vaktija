# Countdown header artwork

Original backgrounds generated separately with the built-in image generation tool and saved as optimized WebP:

- `mosque-horizon-dark.webp`: emerald night, low hills, illuminated mosque and crescent.
- `mosque-horizon-light.webp`: ivory daylight, sage hills, green mosque and apricot sun.
- `mosque-horizon-mobile-dark.webp` and `mosque-horizon-mobile-light.webp`: 4:3 phone compositions with the shores closer together and the waterline higher.

The home theme tokens select the image for the theme and viewport. Phone widths use the 4:3 artwork and align the progress bar with its higher shoreline; larger viewports keep the wide panorama. A theme-specific central overlay protects the countdown and progress arc.

The dark asset was replaced in the shared workspace during verification; that updated version is retained. The prompt below records the initial dark generation.

## Dark image prompt

Create an original background illustration for a prayer countdown panel, wide 3:1 panorama, NO text, NO numbers, NO progress arc, NO interface, NO border. Clean stylized vector-like landscape illustration, simple layered silhouettes and very gentle gradients, crisp contours, refined Islamic miniature sensibility, NOT a photograph, NOT photorealistic, no texture or brushwork. Match an icon family of small emerald mountain valleys, a winding river, Ottoman mosque domes and slender minarets, and restrained warm gold celestial discs. A small graceful mosque and two cypress trees sit at the far LOWER LEFT, distant hills run only along the LOWER QUARTER, a tranquil river reflects restrained gold near the bottom edge, and a small celestial detail sits at the far lower right above a hill. Most of the image, including the entire upper two-thirds and central half, must be exceptionally quiet nearly plain sky, with no objects behind the future countdown. Keep the scene sparse and calm with clear uncluttered center. No stars, sparkles, repeating ornament, detailed buildings, or people. DARK THEME: deep midnight emerald sky around #052a25, layered dark forest and muted teal hills, little warm amber windows in the mosque, a soft pale gold crescent low at the right edge. Restrained sage and antique gold accents, nighttime stillness. Low contrast across the terrain. Broad smooth dark emerald negative space. The scene is original artwork, not a screenshot.
