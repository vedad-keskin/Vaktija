# Instruction notes for the current duas — review draft

**Status:** For review only. The notes proposed here have not been added as card instructions.

This audit covers the **57 cards currently in the app's dua categories**. An instruction note is useful when the source gives a step, sequence, or repetition that the title and recited words do not already explain. The proposed BS/EN notes below are **editorial summaries for review**, not published dua translations or verbatim quotations. The Arabic, transcription, and meaning of each dua are outside this proposal.

**Result:** 2 recommended notes, 12 cases to decide, 2 notes already present, and 41 cards needing no new note. HM-214 is a separate instruction-only source entry, not an app card.

## Recommended

### [ ] HM-25 — Dova poslije ezana · Ezan

**Why:** The preceding Hisnul Muslim entries say to answer the ezan and then send salavat upon the Prophet before this dua. Those steps are missing from the current card. HM-23 also gives a separate response after the mujezin's testimony during the ezan; that wording is not currently a card. HM-24 does not prescribe a particular salavat wording.

**Proposed note (editorial):**

- **BS:** Nakon što odgovoriš na ezan, prouči salavat na Poslanika, pa ovu dovu.
- **EN:** After replying to the adhan, recite blessings on the Prophet, then this supplication.

**Sources:** BS entries 22–25 on [page 13](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=13) and [page 14](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=14); EN [HM-22](https://sunnah.com/hisn:22), [HM-23](https://sunnah.com/hisn:23), [HM-24](https://sunnah.com/hisn:24), [HM-25](https://sunnah.com/hisn:25).

### [ ] HM-178 / `hisn-178-forgot` — Dova kada se sjetimo da smo počeli jesti · Post i hrana

**Why:** Both editions say to use this wording **if one forgot to say “Bismillah” at the start of the meal**, as soon as one remembers. The current title says only that the person remembered starting to eat, so the missing condition is worth stating.

**Proposed note (editorial):**

- **BS:** Ako na početku obroka zaboraviš proučiti “Bismillāhi”, prouči ovu dovu čim se sjetiš.
- **EN:** If you forget to say “Bismillāh” at the start of a meal, recite this supplication when you remember.

**Sources:** [BS, entry 168](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=64); [EN, HM-178](https://sunnah.com/hisn:178).

## Needs a decision

### [ ] HM-79 — Sejjidul-istigfar · Opće

**Source detail:** This appears among the morning and evening remembrances. The English entry adds that it is recited with conviction; its promised outcome is a report, not part of the recited words.

**If you want a contextual note (editorial):**

- **BS:** Uči se ujutro i navečer, s uvjerenjem u značenje dove.
- **EN:** Recite in the morning and evening with conviction in the meaning of the supplication.

**Sources:** [BS, entry 77](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=34); [EN, HM-79](https://sunnah.com/hisn:79).

### [ ] HM-84 — Dova za oprost i zaštitu · Opće

**Source detail:** Both editions place this dua in the morning and evening chapter, but the entry itself gives no extra step or count. A note would supply context missing from the general card title.

**If you want a contextual note (editorial):**

- **BS:** Uči se ujutro i navečer.
- **EN:** Recite in the morning and evening.

**Sources:** [BS, entry 80](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=35); [EN, HM-84](https://sunnah.com/hisn:84).

### [ ] HM-86 — Dova za zaštitu Allahovim imenom · Opće

**Source detail:** Both editions specify **three recitations** in the morning and evening. The current BS/EN meanings reproduce the published count; an instruction note could present it separately above the transcription.

**If you want an instruction note (editorial):**

- **BS:** Prouči ovu dovu tri puta ujutro i tri puta navečer.
- **EN:** Recite this supplication three times in the morning and three times in the evening.

**Sources:** [BS, entry 82](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=36); [EN, HM-86](https://sunnah.com/hisn:86).

### [ ] HM-97 — Utočište u Allahovim riječima · Opće

**Source detail:** English HM-97 specifies **three recitations in the evening**. The Bosnian edition's matching wording is entry 79 and says three times in its morning and evening chapter. The same Arabic wording appears elsewhere for stopping at a place; the selected card follows HM-97's evening context.

**If you want an instruction note (editorial):**

- **BS:** Navečer prouči ovu dovu tri puta.
- **EN:** Recite this supplication three times in the evening.

**Sources:** [BS, entry 79](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=35); [EN, HM-97](https://sunnah.com/hisn:97).

### [ ] HM-20 — Dova pri ulasku u džamiju · Džamija

**Source detail:** The [published English Hisnul Muslim edition](https://risala.prh.gov.sa/en/content/51) says to enter with the right foot before reciting. The linked Bosnian edition and photographed page give the dua without that extra step.

**If you want an instruction note (editorial):**

- **BS:** Pri ulasku u džamiju zakorači desnom nogom, pa prouči ovu dovu.
- **EN:** Enter the mosque with your right foot, then recite this supplication.

### [ ] HM-21 — Dova pri izlasku iz džamije · Džamija

**Source detail:** The [same published English edition](https://risala.prh.gov.sa/en/content/51) says to leave with the left foot before reciting. The linked Bosnian edition and photographed page give the dua without that extra step.

**If you want an instruction note (editorial):**

- **BS:** Pri izlasku iz džamije zakorači lijevom nogom, pa prouči ovu dovu.
- **EN:** Leave the mosque with your left foot, then recite this supplication.

### [ ] HM-191 / `hisn-191-animal` — Dova kad kupimo neku životinju · Životinje

**Source detail:** The same dua is used when buying a camel; the source also describes holding the top of **the camel's hump**. The Bosnian edition parenthetically extends this to another animal, but the action cannot be stated as a general step for every animal. The present card title covers animals broadly.

**If you want a camel-specific note (editorial):**

- **BS:** Ako kupiš devu, uzmi je za vrh grbe i prouči ovu dovu.
- **EN:** If you buy a camel, hold the top of its hump and recite this supplication.

**Recommendation:** Add this only with clear camel-specific wording; otherwise leave the general animal card without an instruction note.

**Sources:** [BS, entry 179](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=68); [EN, HM-191](https://sunnah.com/hisn:191).

### [ ] HM-195 — Dova na sijelu · Sijelo

**Source detail:** Ibn Omer reports that the Prophet said this dua **one hundred times in a single gathering before rising**. This reports a practice; it does not say that every reader must use that count. The English rendering of HM-195 omits the number, but the [English Tirmidhi narration](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3434) includes it.

**If you want a contextual note (editorial):**

- **BS:** Prenosi se da je Poslanik ovu dovu izgovarao stotinu puta tokom jednog sijela.
- **EN:** It is reported that the Prophet recited this supplication one hundred times in one sitting.

**Recommendation:** Keep it out of the current “instruction” treatment unless the card can present it clearly as a **reported practice**, not a required count.

**Sources:** [BS, entry 183](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=69); EN [HM-195](https://sunnah.com/hisn:195), [Jamiʿ at-Tirmidhi 3434](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3434).

### [ ] HM-168 — Dova prilikom grmljavine · Vrijeme

**Source detail:** The report says Abdullah ibn Zubejr stopped speaking when he heard thunder and recited this dua. The English source explicitly identifies it as his statement/practice. It should not be phrased as a universal requirement to stop talking.

**If you want a contextual note (editorial):**

- **BS:** Prenosi se da bi Abdullah ibn Zubejr, kada bi čuo grmljavinu, prekinuo razgovor i proučio ovu dovu.
- **EN:** It is reported that Abdullah ibn Zubayr would stop speaking when he heard thunder and recite this supplication.

**Recommendation:** Leave it off the instruction card unless reported practices have their own clearly labelled note style.

**Sources:** [BS, entry 159](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=61); [EN, HM-168](https://sunnah.com/hisn:168).

### [ ] HM-18 — Dova pri ulasku u kuću · Kuća

**Source detail:** The English Hisn entry says to greet the people present after the recited words. The Bosnian entry prints the dua without that extra sentence.

**If you want an instruction note (editorial):**

- **BS:** Nakon ove dove nazovi selam ukućanima.
- **EN:** After this supplication, greet those at home with salam.

**Sources:** [BS, entry 18](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=12); [EN, HM-18](https://sunnah.com/hisn:18).

### [ ] HM-207 — Putna dova · Putovanje

**Source detail:** Both editions say to recite the travel supplication again on returning and add a short phrase. HM-217 separately provides a return supplication containing that phrase. The card currently shows the travel wording only.

**If you want an instruction note (editorial):**

- **BS:** Pri povratku ponovo prouči putnu dovu i dodaj završnu dovu navedenu u izvoru.
- **EN:** On returning, recite the travel supplication again and add the closing words given in the source.

**Sources:** [BS, entry 195](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=75); [EN, HM-207](https://sunnah.com/hisn:207).

### [ ] HM-217 — Dova pri povratku s puta · Putovanje

**Source detail:** Both editions describe the return wording at each elevation. The current card includes the three opening takbirs, but does not say where to repeat them.

**If you want an instruction note (editorial):**

- **BS:** Pri povratku s puta, na svakom uzvišenju prouči tekbir tri puta, pa ovu dovu.
- **EN:** On the return journey, say the takbir three times at each elevation, then recite this supplication.

**Sources:** [BS, entry 205](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=79); [EN, HM-217](https://sunnah.com/hisn:217).
