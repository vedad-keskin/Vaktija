# Hisnul Muslim — selected full duas (BS / EN)

This file grows one approved category at a time. It currently contains the clothing entries (HM-5, 9, 6, 7), the toilet entries (HM-10–11), the Abdest entries (HM-12–13), the mosque entries (HM-19–21), the adhan entry (HM-25), the sleeping entries (HM-111, 1, 114, 113, 112), the three marriage entries, the gathering entries (HM-195–196), the animal entries, the weather entries (HM-166, 168, 169, 172–175), the fasting and food entries (HM-176–186), the home entries (HM-16, 18), the travel entries (HM-206–212, 215–217), the general entries (HM-79, 84, 86, 97, 188), the illness entries (HM-147, 148, 150–152, 243), the death entries (HM-153–163, 165), and the Hajj and Umrah entries (HM-234–239). The [complete title checklist](README-titles.md) remains an editorial selection aid; its suggested labels are not published translations.

### HM-5 · Dova pri odijevanju odjeće / When wearing a garment

**Arapski / Arabic**

<p lang="ar" dir="rtl">الحمدُ للهِ الّذي كَساني هذا الثّوب وَرَزَقَنيه مِنْ غَـيـْرِ حَولٍ مِنّي وَلا قـوّة</p>

**English — transliteration (Sunnah.com 5):** Alḥamdu lillāhil-ladhī kasānī hādhā (aththawba) wa razaqanīhi min ghayri hawlim-minnī wa lā quwwah.

**English — meaning (Sunnah.com 5):** Praise is to Allah Who has clothed me with this (garment) and provided it for me, though I was powerless myself and incapable

### HM-6 · Dova pri odijevanju nove odjeće / When wearing a new garment

**Arapski / Arabic**

<p lang="ar" dir="rtl">اللّهُـمَّ لَـكَ الحَـمْـدُ أنْـتَ كَسَـوْتَنيهِ، أََسْأََلُـكَ مِـنْ خَـيرِهِ وَخَـيْرِ مَا صُنِعَ لَـه، وَأَعوذُ بِكَ مِـنْ شَـرِّهِ وَشَـرِّ مـا صُنِعَ لَـهُ</p>

**Bosanski — transkripcija (adapted):** Allāhumme leke-l-ḥamdu, Ente kesevtenīhi. Es’ eluke min ḫajrihi ve ḫajri mā ṣuni‘a lehu. Ve e‘ūḏu bike min šerrihi ve šerri mā ṣuni‘a lehu.

**Bosanski — prijevod (IslamHouse 6):** Allahu moj, Tebi hvala. Ti si me ovim odjenuo. Molim Te da mi u tome daš dobro i dobro u onom zbog čega je spravljena, a utječem Ti se od protiv zla njegova i zla onoga zbog čega je spravljena.

**English — transliteration (Sunnah.com 6):** Allāhumma lakal-ḥamdu 'anta kasawtanīhi, 'as'aluka min khayrihi wa khayri mā ṣuni`a lahu, wa 'a`oothu bika min sharrihi wa sharri mā ṣuni`a lahu.

**English — meaning (Sunnah.com 6):** O Allah, praise is to You. You have clothed me. I ask You for its goodness and the goodness of what it has been made for, and I seek Your protection from the evil of it and the evil of what it has been made for.

**Sources:** [Bosnian PDF, page 9](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=9) · [English and Arabic](https://sunnah.com/hisn:6)

## Džamija / Mosque

<a id="hm-19"></a>
### HM-19 · Dova pri odlasku u džamiju / When going to the mosque

**Arapski / Arabic**

<p lang="ar" dir="rtl">اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْ فِي قَلْبِي نُورًا، وَفِي لِسَانِي نُورًا، وَاجْعَلْ فِي سَمْعِي نُورًا، وَاجْعَلْ فِي بَصَرِي نُورًا، وَاجْعَلْ مِنْ خَلْفِي نُورًا، وَمِنْ أَمَامِي نُورًا، وَاجْعَلْ مِنْ فَوْقِي نُورًا، وَمِنْ تَحْتِي نُورًا، اللَّهُمَّ أَعْطِنِي نُورًا</p>

**Bosanski — transkripcija (2017 edition, page 21):** Allāhumme-dž‘al fī qalbī nūren, ve fī lisānī nūren, ve-dž‘al fī sem‘ī nūren, ve-dž‘al fī besarī nūren, ve-dž‘al min halfī nūren ve min emāmī nūren, ve-dž‘al min fevqī nūren ve min tahtī nūren. Allāhumme ‘atinī nūren.

**Bosanski — prijevod (IslamHouse 19):** Allahu moj, podari mi u srce svjetlo i u jezik svjetlo, osvijetli mi sluh i vid, daruj mi svjetlo iza mene i ispred mene, podari mi iznad mene i ispod mene svjetlo. Allahu moj, svjetlo mi daruj.

**English — transliteration (Sahih Muslim 1799):** Allahummaj'al fi qalbi nuran, wa fi lisani nuran waj'al fi sam'i nuran waj'al fi basari nuran waj'al min khalfi nuran wa min amami nuran waj'al min fawqi nuran wa min tahti nuran. Allahumma a'tini nura.

**English — meaning (Hisnul Muslim 19, short wording):** O Allah, place within my heart light, and upon my tongue light, and within my ears light, and within my eyes light, and place behind me light and in front of me light and above me light and beneath me light. O Allah, bestow upon me light.

**Sources:** [Bosnian PDF, page 12](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=12) · [English and Arabic](https://ahadith.co.uk/permalink-hadith-27/hisnulmuslim-dua-12) · [English transliteration](https://www.alim.org/hadith/sahih-muslim/6/1799/)

## Ezan / Adhan

<a id="hm-22"></a>
### HM-22 · Odgovor pri slušanju ezana / Responding to the adhan

### HM-236 · Dova prilikom boravka na Saffi i Mervi / When at Mount Safa and Mount Marwah

**Bosanski — uputa (2017 edition, pages 136–137):** Pri prilasku Saffi prouči ajet. Zatim se popni na Saffu, okreni prema Kibli i prouči zikr tri puta, upućujući svoju dovu između ponavljanja. Isto učini na Mervi.

**Bosanski — transkripcija (prva rečenica prilagođena arapskom tekstu; ostatak prema fotografiji 2017 edition, pages 136–137):** Inne-ṣ-Ṣafā ve-l-Mervete min še‘ā’irillāh. Lā ilāhe illellāhu va-l-Lāhu ekber, lā ilāhe illellāhu vaḥdehū lā šerīke lehū, lehū-l-mulku ve lehū-l-ḥamdu ve huve ‘alā kulli šej’in qadīr, lā ilāhe illellāhu vaḥdehū, endžeze va‘dehū ve neṣare ‘abdehū, ve hezeme-l-aḥzābe vaḥdehū.

**Bosanski — prijevod (2017 edition, pages 136–137; početak zikra dopunjen):** Safa i Merva su Allahova časna mjesta. Nema boga osim Allaha i Allah je najveći. Nema boga osim Allaha, Jedinoga, Koji Sebi ravnoga nema. Njemu pripada sva vlast i zahvalnost i On je iznad svega. Nema boga osim Allaha, Jedinoga, Koji ispunjava Svoje obećanje i pomaže Svojem robu, i sve protivnike Sam porazi.

**English — transliteration (Sunnah.com 236, phrase outside the verse omitted):** Innaṣ-Ṣafā wa ‘l-Marwata min sha&#96;ā'irillāh. Lā ilāha illallāh, Allāhu Akbar. Lā ilāha illallāhu waḥdahu lā sharīka lah, lahu ‘l-mulku wa lahu ‘l-ḥamdu wa huwa &#96;alā kulli shay'in qadīr, lā ilāha illallāhu waḥdahu, anjaza wa&#96;dahu, wa naṣara &#96;abdahu, wa hazama ‘l-aḥzāba waḥdah.

**English — meaning (Sunnah.com 236, phrase outside the verse omitted):** Surely Safa and Marwah are among the signs of Allah. There is none worthy of worship but Allah, and Allah is the Most Great. None has the right to be worshipped but Allah alone, Who has no partner, His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He is Able to do all things. None has the right to be worshipped but Allah alone, He fulfilled His Promise, He aided His slave, and He alone defeated the Confederates.

**Sources:** [Bosnian PDF, pages 83–84](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=83) · [English and Arabic](https://sunnah.com/hisn:236)
