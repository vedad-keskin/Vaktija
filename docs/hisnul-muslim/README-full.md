# Hisnul Muslim — selected full duas (BS / EN)

This file grows one approved category at a time. It currently contains the toilet entries (HM-10–11), the Abdest entries (HM-12–13), the adhan entry (HM-25), the three marriage entries, and the weather entries (HM-166, 168, 169, 172–175). The [complete title checklist](README-titles.md) remains an editorial selection aid; its suggested labels are not published translations.

## Toalet / Toilet

<a id="hm-10"></a>
### HM-10 · Dova prije ulaska u zahod / Before entering the bathroom

**Arapski / Arabic**

<p lang="ar" dir="rtl">بِسْمِ الله اللّهُـمَّ إِنِّـي أَعـوذُ بِـكَ مِـنَ الْخُـبْثِ وَالْخَبائِث</p>

**Bosanski — transkripcija (adapted):** Bismillāhi, Allāhumme innī e‘ūḏu bike mine-l-ẖubṯi ve-l-ẖabā’iṯi.

**Bosanski — prijevod (IslamHouse 10):** U ime Allaha, Allahu moj, prizivam Te protiv zla muških i ženskih šejtana.

**English — transliteration (Sunnah.com 10):** [Bismillāhi] Allāhumma 'innī 'a`ūdhu bika minal-khubthi walkhabā'ith.

**English — meaning (Sunnah.com 10):** [In the Name of Allah]. O Allah, I seek protection in You from evil and the evil ones (or, from the evil male and female Jinn).

**Sources:** [Bosnian PDF, page 14](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=14) · [English and Arabic](https://sunnah.com/hisn:10)

<a id="hm-13"></a>
### HM-13 · Dova nakon uzimanja abdesta / Upon completing the ablution

**Arapski / Arabic**

<p lang="ar" dir="rtl">أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لا إِلَـهَ إِلاّ اللهُ وَحْدَهُ لا شَريـكَ لَـهُ وَأَشْهَدُأَنَّ مُحَمّـداً عَبْـدُهُ وَرَسـولُـه</p>

**Bosanski — transkripcija (adapted):** Ešhedu en lā ilāhe illellāhu vaḥdehu lā šerīke lehu ve ešhedu enne Muḥammeden ‘abduhu ve resūluhu.

**Bosanski — prijevod (IslamHouse 13):** Svjedočim da nema boga osim Allaha i svjedočim da je Muhammed Njegov rob i Njegov Poslanik.

**English — transliteration (Sunnah.com 13):** 'Ash-hadu 'an lā 'ilāha 'illallāhu waḥdahu lā sharīka lahu wa 'ash-hadu 'anna Muḥammadan `abduhu wa Rasūluhu.

**English — meaning (Sunnah.com 13):** I bear witness that none has the right to be worshipped but Allah alone, Who has no partner; and I bear witness that Muhammad is His slave and His Messenger.

**Sources:** [Bosnian PDF, page 14](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=14) · [English and Arabic](https://sunnah.com/hisn:13)

## Ezan / Adhan

<a id="hm-25"></a>
### HM-25 · Dova poslije ezana / Supplication after the adhan

**Arapski / Arabic**

<p lang="ar" dir="rtl">اللّهُـمَّ رَبَّ هَذِهِ الدّعْـوَةِ التّـامَّة وَالصّلاةِ القَـائِمَة آتِ محَـمَّداً الوَسيـلةَ وَالْفَضـيلَة وَابْعَـثْه مَقـامـاً مَحـموداً الَّذي وَعَـدْتَه</p>

**Bosanski — transkripcija (adapted):** Allāhumme rabbe hāḏihi-d-da‘veti-t-tāmmeti ve-ṣ-ṣalāti-l-qā’imeti āti Muḥammedeni-l-vesīlete ve-l-faḍīlete ve-b‘aṯhu meqāmen maḥmūdeni-l-leḏī ve‘adtehu.

**English — transliteration (Sunnah.com 25):** Allāhumma rabba hādhihi 'd-da'wati 't-tāmmah waṣ-ṣalāti 'l-qā'imah, 'āti Muhammadani 'l-wasīlata walfaḍīlata, wab 'ath-hu maqāma 'm-mahmūdani 'l-ladhī wa`adtahu.

**Bosanski — prijevod (IslamHouse 25):** Uči se: Allahu moj, Gospodaru ove cjelovite molbe i namaza koji se upravo uspostavlja, podari Muhammedu veliki stepen u Džennetu, posebno dobro i uzdigni ga na uzvišeno mjesto, koje si mu obećao.

**English — meaning (Sunnah.com 25):** O Allah , Lord of this perfect call and established prayer. Grant Muhammad the intercession and favor, and raise him to the honored station You have promised him.

**Sources:** [Bosnian PDF, page 18](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=18) · [English and Arabic](https://sunnah.com/hisn:25)

## Brak / Marriage

### HM-192 · Dova prije spolnog odnosa u braku / Before sexual intercourse with the wife

**Arapski / Arabic**

<p lang="ar" dir="rtl">بِسْمِ الله اللّهُـمَّ جَنِّبْنا الشَّيْـطانَ، وَجَنِّبِ الشَّـيْطانَ ما رَزَقْـتَنا</p>

**English — meaning (Sunnah.com 192):** With the Name of Allah. O Allah, keep the Devil away from us, and keep the Devil away from that which You provide for us.

**Sources:** [Bosnian PDF, page 68](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=68) · [English and Arabic](https://sunnah.com/hisn:192)
<a id="hm-191"></a>
### HM-191 · Dova koju mladoženja uči sebi / On the wedding night or when buying an animal

**Arapski / Arabic**

<p lang="ar" dir="rtl">اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ خَيْرَهَا، وَخَيْرَ مَا جَبَلْتَهَا عَلَيْهِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّهَا، وَشَرِّ مَا جَبَلْتَهَا عَلَيْهِ</p>

**Bosanski — transkripcija (adapted):** Allāhumme innī es’eluke ẖajrehā ve ẖajre mā džebeltehā ‘alejhi ve e‘ūḏu bike min šerrihā ve šerri mā džebeltehā ‘alejhi.

**Bosanski — prijevod (IslamHouse 179):** Allahu, molim Te da mi u njoj (ili njemu) podariš dobro i da ono čemu je ona (ili on) po naravi sklon(a), bude dobro. I utičem Ti se od njenoga (njegovoga) zla i zla onoga čemu je po prirodi sklon(a).

**English — transliteration (Sunnah.com 191):** Allāhumma innī as'aluka khayrahā wa khayra mā jabaltahā \`alayh, wa a\`ūdhu bika min sharrihā wa sharri mā jabaltahā \`alayh.

**English — meaning (Sunnah.com 191):** O Allah, I ask You for the goodness of her, and the goodness upon which You have created her, and I seek refuge in You from the evil of her, and from the evil upon which You have created her.

**Sources:** [Bosnian PDF, page 68](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=68) · [English and Arabic](https://sunnah.com/hisn:191)

### HM-169 · Kišne dove / For rain

**Arapski / Arabic**

<p lang="ar" dir="rtl">اللّهُمَّ اسْقِـنا غَيْـثاً مُغيـثاً مَريئاً مُريـعاً، نافِعـاً غَيْـرَ ضار، عاجِـلاً غَـيْرَ آجِل</p>

**English — meaning (Sunnah.com 169):** O Allah, shower upon us abundant rain, beneficial not harmful, swiftly and not delayed.

<a id="hm-174"></a>
### HM-174 · Dova za prestanak velikih kiša / Asking for clear skies

**Arapski / Arabic**

<p lang="ar" dir="rtl">اللّهُمَّ حَوالَيْنا وَلا عَلَيْـنا، اللّهُمَّ عَلى الآكـامِ وَالظِّـراب، وَبُطـونِ الأوْدِية، وَمَنـابِتِ الشَّجـر</p>

**Bosanski — transkripcija (adapted):** Allāhumme ḥavālejnā ve lā ‘alejnā. Allāhumme ‘ale-l-ākāmi ve-ẓ-ẓirābi, ve buṭūni-l-evdijeti, ve menābiti-š-šedžeri.

**Bosanski — prijevod (IslamHouse 165):** Allahu, izvan naše okoline spusti ove kiše, a ne na nas. Allahu, na zaravni i brežuljke, u utrobe dolina i korjeništa drveća (isprazni kišne oblake).

**English — transliteration (Sunnah.com 174):** Allāhumma ḥawālaynā wa lā `alaynā. Allāhumma `ala ‘l-ākāmi waẓ-ẓirāb, wa buṭūni ‘l-awdiyati, wa manābitish-shajar.

**English — meaning (Sunnah.com 174):** O Allah, let the rain fall around us and not upon us, O Allah, (let it fall) on the pastures, hills, valleys, and the roots of trees.

**Sources:** [Bosnian PDF, page 66](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=66) · [English and Arabic](https://sunnah.com/hisn:174)

<a id="hm-175"></a>
### HM-175 · Dova prilikom viđenja mladoga mjeseca / Upon sighting the crescent moon

**Arapski / Arabic**

<p lang="ar" dir="rtl">اللهُ أَكْـبَر، اللّهُمَّ أَهِلَّـهُ عَلَيْـنا بِالأمْـنِ وَالإيمـان، والسَّلامَـةِ والإسْلام، وَالتَّـوْفيـقِ لِما تُحِـبُّ رَبَّنـا وَتَـرْضـى، رَبُّنـا وَرَبُّكَ الله</p>

**Bosanski — transkripcija (adapted):** Allāhu ekber, Allāhumme ehillehu ‘alejnā bi-l-emni ve-l-īmāni ve-s-selāmeti ve-l-islāmi ve-t-tevfīqi limā tuḥibbu ve terḍā, rabbenā ve rabbuke-llāhu.

**Bosanski — prijevod (IslamHouse 166):** Allah je najveći, Allahu, učini da nam ovaj mjesec nastupi u znaku sigurnosti i imana, mira i islama, te pomoći u onome što Ti voliš i čime si zadovoljan. Naš Gospodar i tvoj Gospodar (Mjeseče) jest Allah.

**English — transliteration (Sunnah.com 175):** Allāhu Akbar, Allāhumma ahillahu `alayna bi ‘l-amni wa ‘l-īmān, was-salāmati wa ‘l-'Islām, wat-tawfīqi limā tuḥibbu Rabbanā wa tarḍā, Rabbunā wa Rabbukallāh.

**English — meaning (Sunnah.com 175):** Allah is the Most Great. O Allah, bring us the new moon with security and Faith, with peace and in Islam, and in harmony with what our Lord loves and what pleases Him. Our Lord and your Lord is Allah.

**Sources:** [Bosnian PDF, page 67](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/bs/ih_books/single1/bs_Hisnul_muslim.pdf#page=67) · [English and Arabic](https://sunnah.com/hisn:175)
